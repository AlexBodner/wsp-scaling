# Does Astra find induction heads on a bigger model, where only a combined recipe works?

Question: Q005. With Python and no method, does Astra find induction heads on models where the known statistic fails or that nobody has published heads for?
Rests on: checks/outputs/medium_recipes.txt on scrambled gpt2-medium, 355M weights. Best pairing and two sums of top sources get 2 of 5, mean true score 0.642. By source gets 0 of 5, 0.243. Summing cosines with the 8 strongest writers gets 4 of 5, 0.842. The ceiling is 0.921. Results are the same at all precisions tried. Peak memory is 5 to 6.5 GB and float32 takes 5 seconds, so the sandbox can do it.
Mechanism: medium has several strong previous-token heads, 5.11 0.988, 4.13 0.955 and 6.7 0.948, so comparing with one source misses heads that read another. A reader that follows the single-source recipe scores near 0.64. One that looks at its output and notices several writers can reach 0.84 or better.
Prediction: Astra's picks score between 0.64 and 0.84 mean true score, overlapping the best-pairing picks more than the combined-writers picks. It doesn't beat 0.842.
Control: the recipes in checks/outputs/medium_recipes.txt are the fixed baselines, and were computed before any Astra run on this model. The main score is the picks' mean true score, because top-5 overlap is coarse: uniform picks from the true top 10 reach 3 of 5 half the time. Also reported: top-5 overlap, picks within 0.02 of the 5th, overlap with each recipe's picks, and chance from a within-layer permutation. Fresh scramble seed 1201. Prompt frozen in prompts/FAMILY_MANIFEST.json, the same text as GPT-2 small's except for shapes, with no example naming a layer. A forward pass voids a run. A run that stopped on memory or time is reported as that, not as a failure of method. Any statistic Astra writes gets rerun with no layer rule on GPT-2 small, medium and distilgpt2.
If it comes out as predicted: on a bigger model Astra's reading is no better than a known recipe it could have remembered, and it may not adapt it. Report those numbers.
